### Reproducibility examples

- **Bootstrap confidence intervals:** seven interval methods, small-sample coverage experiments, and checks against published numerical targets.
- **Cafe replenishment:** three replenishment policies across 54 settings, with separate calibration and evaluation runs.
- **Truncated-normal simulation:** an independent audit of 27 printed acceptance probabilities and 47 Monte Carlo method-setting pairs. Twenty-six printed values agree at three decimal places. The report preserves the localized discrepancy and its independent numerical checks.

Each repository identifies its source, implementation, calculation settings and results.
